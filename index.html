When to come in

Four signs
it's time.

  • 01
    Tread down to the wear barsSmall raised bars sit inside the grooves. Once the tread is level with them, wet grip drops sharply.
  • 02
    Cracks or a bulge on the sidewallA bulge means the inner structure is damaged. Don't wait for the next service.
  • 03
    Steering shakes at speedUsually a balance problem. Road-force balancing is built to find it.
  • 04
    Slow cranking in the morningA weak battery gives warnings before it fails. A quick test tells you where it stands.
